# Site structure and texts (v1, approved)

Concept: **"Snow to Sun" + "Still Life"**.
The *Me* tab opens quietly (portrait in an arch, "Still blooming"), then turns into a cinematic journey
through light and places. Between the photo chapters sit realistic still-life interludes: one peony on a
linen table, photographed at a different stage each time (tight bud → opening → full bloom → a fallen
petal). The last one is the open question.

Accent colour: powdered terracotta `#c08a78`. Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (headings), Manrope (text),
Pinyon Script (only the signature).

Everything below is English (the main language). After you approve it, I translate to RU / IT / DE.
Lines in *[brackets]* are notes for you, not site text.
